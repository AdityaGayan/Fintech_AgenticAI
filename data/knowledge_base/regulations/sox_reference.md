# SOX reference

This project reference summarizes software controls commonly associated with
financial reporting systems. It is not the official Sarbanes-Oxley Act or legal
advice.

- Maintain reliable audit trails for changes to financial data and reporting
  logic.
- Separate duties for development, approval, deployment, and production access.
- Review access regularly and remove unnecessary privileges promptly.
- Preserve evidence of control operation, change approvals, testing, and
  exceptions.
- Protect the integrity, availability, and retention of records used for
  financial reporting.
