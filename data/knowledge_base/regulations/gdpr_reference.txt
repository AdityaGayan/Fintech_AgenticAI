Title: GDPR — Educational Reference Summary
Type: Compliance reference
Status: Educational summary; not legal advice.
Source: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/

Core principles from Article 5
- Process personal data lawfully, fairly, and transparently.
- Collect it for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes.
- Limit collection to data that is adequate, relevant, and necessary.
- Keep data accurate and correct inaccurate data.
- Retain data only as long as needed for the stated purpose or a lawful obligation.
- Protect confidentiality, integrity, and availability using appropriate safeguards.
- Be able to demonstrate accountability for these principles.

Security themes from Article 32
Security measures should be appropriate to the risk and may include pseudonymization or encryption, the ability to maintain confidentiality/integrity/availability/resilience, timely restoration after an incident, and regular testing and evaluation of safeguards.

How this application uses the reference
The SDLC recommender may use these themes when requirements mention personal data, privacy, consent, retention, deletion, data minimization, access controls, encryption, breach response, or privacy-by-design. The retrieved text is a planning aid and is not a legal determination.
