Title: SOX Section 404 — Educational Reference Summary
Type: Compliance reference
Status: Educational summary; not legal advice.
Source: https://www.sec.gov/news/extra/soxpress.htm

Control objective
Section 404 concerns management's assessment of internal control over financial reporting. Public-company reporting obligations can also include an independent auditor's attestation, subject to applicable rules and exemptions.

Control themes
- Define financial-reporting risks, key controls, control owners, and evidence requirements.
- Restrict access to financial systems and separate incompatible duties.
- Review changes to applications, data, configurations, and reporting logic before release.
- Reconcile important records, investigate exceptions, and preserve an auditable trail.
- Monitor control operation, document testing, record deficiencies, and track remediation.
- Retain support for management conclusions and coordinate with internal and external audit activities.

How this application uses the reference
The SDLC recommender may use these themes when requirements mention financial reporting, accounting data, audit evidence, segregation of duties, reconciliations, approvals, change control, or control testing. The retrieved text is a planning aid and is not an assessment of SOX applicability.
